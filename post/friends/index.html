Phoebes Ex-Partner
S03E14 2024.7.27

Interesting, I just find that the main characters of this three episodes are different, and today it’s Phoebes, another star in Friends.

To be honest, Phoebes is a werid person, who believes in ghosts, often seems unreasonable(不近人情的)， and her mom suicided, her stepfather in jail. I have to say, the character setting is unbelievable.

Phoebes is a guitarist, singing in the bar, where is a chatting place in Friends. All the songs are created by herself, including lyrics and tunes.

Nonetheless, the songs aren’t traditional songs, which means that a few listeners like them, or at least can’t comprehend them.

And there are another guitarist, named Leslie, come to the bar to sing the traditional songs. She is the ex-partner of Phoebes, trying to repair the broken relationship with Phoebes. By Phoebes’s words, Leslie abandoned her to write jingles(广告歌), a kind of song on TV shows to boost sales, which are popular among public and lucrative(我最近背的！获利多的). However, Phoebes depreciate(也是我背过的！轻视、贬低) jingles, and that’s why they broke up.

At first, I wrongly judge the theme of this episode as disaggrement between profession and business, which is acceptable for me.

Leslie express her attitude by words like “getting kind of sick of jingles(变得对广告歌有些厌倦)”, by actions like calling again and again even if being hung up repeatedly, wanting to get back together. Initially, Phoebes is very cold, hard, unyielding(冷淡、冷酷、决不让步), and nothing can penetrate her icy exterior(没有东西能穿透她冷硬的外壳), for the heart-breaking feeling that the best friends betrayed.

However, an old song that they song commonly in the past unfreezes Phoebes’s heart. They are friends again. But as the title reads, “ex-partern”, Leslie betray again, selling Phoebes’s songs she love most.

It’s a sad but realistic story.

The Morning After
S03E16 2024.7.29

Though there isn’t subtitles in S03E15, this episode recaps previous plot so I get a rough konwledge about what has happened.

Ross want Rachel to quit her job, because Rachel is so busy that he doesn’t feel like having a girlfriend.

It resembles the plot in The queen wears prada, the couple quarrels over someone’s absence in the others’ birthday or anniversary(周年纪念日) dinner.

Rachel propose that they just should “take a break” from them, which means “having a rest”. Nonetheless, some conincidences results in a true “breaking-up”.

Polysemy(一词多义) is an interesting phenomenon in linguistics(语言学), which can bring about many humorous or meaningful coincidence.

Rachel feels regretful, and the message what she leaves is “I’ve been trying to reach you all night. I feel awful. Please, Ross , you’ve got to know , there’s nothing between me and Mark. This whole breakup thing is just stupid. I’m sorry I put you through it.”
However, just the night they be apart, Ross has sex with another women. No matter what the reason is, he has took a misstep, irreparable(无法修复的). Rachel’s love has been felt, so Ross trys to be honest to tell her what dump mistake he has made, but his friends Joe and Chandler insist that being honest is stuff making troubles and benefiting nobody and just hurting Rachel.

It’s irreparable. Conceal(隐瞒) is a guilt which emerges on Ross’s memory from time to time, yet being honest is an injure which cut in Rachel’s heart forever.

Ross is convinced and try hard to conceal. But truth will come to light sooner or later(纸包不住火). “You’re a totally different person to me now. I used to think of you as somebody taht would never, ever hurt me. Ever. Now I can’t stop picturing you with her. I can’t. It doesn’t matter what you say or what you do. It’s just changed everything. Forever. "

A Chick And A Duck
S03E21 2024.8.15

Lovelorn(失恋的) Joe becomes sort of dull(沉闷的). One day he watchs the news where someone gives chicks as festival presents. What’s funny is that Joe deems the new as a commercial ad(商业广告), and he make a call to take a chick home.

A little puzzled. I don’t know why Joe like chicks. Joe says bacause chicks are cute. Maybe he want to shift his attention in order to get rid of lovesick.

There is a humorous plot. Joe goes back home from work and want to meet other actors for drinks. Nevertheless Chandler feels unfair because he stuck home all day to accompany with their chick but Joe expects to go off gallivanting with his friends(想要和朋友出去花天酒地). They have a small quarrel.

This dialogue is clever: “Did you notice that ever since we got this chick, we have been fighting a lot more than we used to? "

From perspective of traditional family, Chandler represents mother who dedicates herself to tending kids, Joe represents father who works hardly to support whole family, and the chick is kid.

In the end, Chandler take a duck home too.

I don’t konw why this episode is titled with “a chick and a duck”, because the duck doesn’t matter at all.
